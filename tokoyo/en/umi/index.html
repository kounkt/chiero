Observation One · 18 pieces

The Sea

Things that are in the water, and the water itself.

A wave crosses the body.
There are parts that move first and parts that move late.
It is the lag that makes them look alive.
Move them together and every one becomes a machine opening and closing.

Ripples and Tide have no body.
Only the wave that crosses is left.

Squid and Shark arrived, and it closed once.
