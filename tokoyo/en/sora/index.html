Observation Two · 7 pieces

The Sky

Things that have left the water.

What is placed here is not how the thing is.
It is how it falls toward us.

Over there the butterfly's wing stays flat the whole time.
It looks short because it is tilting toward us.

What you see in Wake is not an orbit either.
It is the track left behind.

Both look that way only when seen from here.
