Observation Four · 7 pieces

Likeness

Points approaching the same ring. Two settling in one place.
A flow appearing and disappearing. Arms leaving the centre empty.

Watching them may bring anxiety, love, money or God to mind.
Here, that feeling of resemblance is called Likeness.

What does looking closely at a shape bring back to you?
The name is left with the person looking.

Try reducing the points.40,000 → 625 → 40 → 1. The equation stays the same.
