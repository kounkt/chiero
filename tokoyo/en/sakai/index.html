Observation Five · 6 pieces

The Boundary

On this side there are lines where we decided that beyond here it is different.

A gate. A crease in paper. An island's shore. A line drawn in sand.

On that side the line was not drawn.
Join the places of equal height and it happens by itself.

The line is not there because it was drawn.
It looks like a line because something changes there.

A snake is a line that is itself one body.
While a bend born at the head travels to the tail, the line becomes a living creature.

Lightning alone draws no line.
It crosses in one go.

Try reducing the points.40,000 → 625 → 40 → 1. The equation stays the same.
