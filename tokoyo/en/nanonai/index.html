Observation Three · 6 pieces

The Nameless

On that side there are no names.

Flower, Shell, Cocoon, Whirl, Sash —
these titles are only borrowed from the nearest thing on this side.

Once a name is fixed, the looking is over.
So the things here are not named all the way.
