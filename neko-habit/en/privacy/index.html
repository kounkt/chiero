CAT HABIT

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Information stored on your device

Your habits, scheduled weekdays, completion records, cat nickname, fish, furniture, selected cat and room are stored on this device. They are used to show records, rewards and your room, and to keep your settings. Saved room layouts and widget and photo display preferences are also managed locally.

Accounts and network access

Cat Habit does not require an account. It does not send habit records to the publisher's servers, and does not include advertising or analytics. Apple and operating-system diagnostic and purchase information is handled according to their respective settings and policies.

In-app purchases and restoration

Purchases and restoration of extra cats, rooms, furnished sets and Cat Habit Plus use Apple's in-app purchase system. Purchase information verified by Apple is used to enable purchased content. This app does not ask for or store card numbers.

Backups

You can save habits, completions and related records to a file. You choose where to save or share it and are responsible for keeping it safe. Importing a file replaces the current records on the device. Purchase entitlements are verified separately with Apple.

Widgets and photo sharing

From version 1.3, widgets store cat, room and furniture images, completion totals and display settings in a local area shared by the app and its widget. Habit titles and your cat's nickname are not shown in widgets. You create photos and choose where to save or send them using the operating system's share sheet. Including the nickname or completed-day count in a photo is off by default. Check the image and destination before sharing. Clearing app records does not delete images already saved or sent elsewhere.

Deleting records

Use Clear records on this device in Settings to delete app records and local recovery data. Delete any backup files saved elsewhere yourself. This action does not delete Apple's purchase history or request a refund.

Retention

Records stay on this device. They may be lost when the app is deleted or you change devices. Back up records you want to keep beforehand.

Support inquiries

If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message to reply and investigate problems. If sending a backup, include only the information needed. Contact the publisher for requests to access, correct or delete personal information.

Contact

For technical issues, include your iPhone model, iOS version and the steps that led to the problem. Do not send card details or your Apple Account password.
