About the app

Cat Habit lets you record small tidying tasks and habits while enjoying a cat's room. It does not guarantee continued tidying, habit formation or health benefits.

Free features and optional purchases

The basic cat and room, habit tracking and furniture earned with fish are free. From version 1.3, the basic widget and photo sharing are also free. Extra cats, rooms and furnished sets are optional one-time purchases. Check the included content and price in the app and Google Play's purchase confirmation screen.

Cat Habit Plus

Optional Plus access provides four widget/photo colors, up to six saved room combinations, and monthly/yearly records. You can switch saved rooms in the widget. Monthly, annual and lifetime plans have the same Plus features. Extra cats and furniture sets are sold separately. Lifetime access is a single payment with no recurring fee. Available plans and prices appear in the app and Google Play confirmation screen.

Monthly and annual subscriptions

Monthly plans charge for one month at a time; annual plans charge for one year at a time. Your Google Account is charged at confirmation. Plans renew for the same period unless cancelled. To stop renewal, cancel before it renews in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Access continues until the current period ends. Habit records and purchased cats and rooms remain after Plus expires. Plus features require an active plan or referral pass.

Fish and restoring purchases

Fish are points earned within the app. They cannot be exchanged for cash or transferred between users. Each tidying task earns a reward once a day. Cat, room, set and Plus purchases can be restored with the same Google Account. A separate backup file is needed to transfer habit records, furniture earned with fish and furniture placement.

Refunds and data

Follow Google Play's process to request a refund for purchases. You are responsible for backups and files saved outside the app. We will provide necessary notices if app updates change the service.

Invitation rewards

A pilot for the first 30 pairs. Accept one invitation within 7 days of first verifying participation with Google and invite up to 3 people. The first activity gives both participants furniture. Activities on 3 separate days within 14 days earn a one-month Plus pass for both. Days need not be consecutive; activity must reach the server on the day. Each person can earn the Plus reward once. A pass runs from claiming until the same date of the next month, or its last day when that date does not exist. There is no automatic renewal or charge. Current Plus members receive alternative furniture; existing subscription fees and periods are unchanged. Self-referrals and duplicate claims are ineligible. Current availability is shown in the app.
