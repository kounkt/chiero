Android privacy policy

Updated: 2026-09-25 · CHIERO Inc.

Information stored on your device

Your habits, scheduled weekdays, completion records, cat nickname, fish, furniture, selected cat and room are stored on this device. They are used to show records, rewards and your room, and to keep your settings. Saved room layouts and widget and photo display preferences are also managed locally.

Accounts and network access

No account is required for ordinary habit tracking. Habit names and contents are not sent to us. When buying or restoring, we verify Google Play purchase proofs on our server. Joining the optional referral program sends participation verification and activity days as described below. We have no advertising or analytics features. Google and operating-system diagnostics follow their own settings and policies.

Optional invitations

After you consent to participation, CHIERO verifies Google sign-in tokens and Play Integrity app/device proofs on Cloudflare servers. Google identity tokens may contain a name and email address; we discard these after verification without storing them. Account and device identifiers are stored as keyed hashes. We retain first participation verification time, referral relationships, time-zone offset, activity days and reward grants/expiry to provide rewards and prevent abuse. The device session is encrypted with Android Keystore. Habit names and contents, cat names, contacts and friends’ names are not sent. IP addresses are hashed for short-term rate limits and deleted after expiry. Google and Cloudflare may process data outside your country.

Deleting invitation data

Settings → Invite a friend → Delete invitation participation disables your participation and deletes referral recording days and furniture information. During operation of the program, we retain minimal keyed user/device hashes, participation and reward-allocation facts, and invitation relationships to prevent repeated claims and preserve the other participant’s earned rewards. This is separate from clearing ordinary habit records. While you participate, we keep information needed to restore your rewards. Contact support for access or deletion requests.

In-app purchases and restoration

Optional cats, rooms, furniture sets and Cat Habit Plus use Google Play Billing. Purchase tokens and product IDs are checked through the Google Play Developer API from our Cloudflare server to verify ownership and expiry. We do not persist purchase tokens or card numbers on our server. You do not enter or store card numbers in this app.

Backups

You can save habits, completions and related records to a file. You choose where to save or share it and are responsible for keeping it safe. Importing a file replaces the current records on the device. Purchase entitlements are verified separately with Google Play.

Widgets and photo sharing

From version 1.3, widgets store cat, room and furniture images, completion totals and display settings in a local area shared by the app and its widget. Habit titles and your cat's nickname are not shown in widgets. You create photos and choose where to save or send them using the operating system's share sheet. Including the nickname or completed-day count in a photo is off by default. Check the image and destination before sharing. Clearing app records does not delete images already saved or sent elsewhere.

Deleting records

Use Clear records on this device in Settings to delete app records and local recovery data. Delete any backup files saved elsewhere yourself. This action does not delete Google Play's purchase history or request a refund.

Retention

Records stay on this device. They may be lost when the app is deleted or you change devices. Back up records you want to keep beforehand.

Support inquiries

If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message to reply and investigate problems. If sending a backup, include only the information needed. Contact the publisher for requests to access, correct or delete personal information.
